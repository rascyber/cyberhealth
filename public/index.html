How it works

Sign up, pair, sync. That's the set-up.

1

Sign up in the CyberHealth app

Register with your HPCSA or SAPC number, add your practice and choose how you like your notes and letters written.

2

Pair your CyberHealth Scribe

Hold the Scribe near your phone and tap Pair. It is linked to your account only, so a lost device can be locked from the app.

3

Sync your consultations

Recordings upload encrypted and match to the patient in your queue. CyberHealth drafts the record; you review, correct and sign.

The CyberHealth Scribe

A recorder built for the consulting room, not a phone propped on the desk.

Most AI scribes need your phone recording on the desk. The Scribe clips on or sits by the patient, records the whole consult clearly, and syncs to the CyberHealth app when you connect, so you never lose a consult to a flat battery or a missed tap.

For employees Terminal in development

One touch to check in. Your SpO₂ and pulse, privately yours.

The CyberHealth terminal is a workplace check-in and check-out point with a built-in finger sensor. Place your finger to clock in or out. If you have joined health screening, the same touch measures your blood oxygen (SpO₂) and pulse rate and sends them to your private CyberHealth app.

Working remotely? Your care team, appointments and the app all work without the terminal.

Finger sensor · concept illustration
  1. Step 1

    Place your finger

    Tap Check in when you arrive or Check out when you leave. The terminal confirms it's you.

  2. Step 2

    Attendance recorded

    Your check-in or check-out is saved straight away, whatever happens next.

  3. Step 3

    Keep still for your readings

    SpO₂ and pulse rate are measured and quality-checked. Poor signal? Try again. Nothing is ever guessed.

  4. Step 4

    Private to you

    Readings land in your CyberHealth app. Share a summary with your doctor or pharmacist only if you choose.

Your readings are yours

Your employer sees your check-in and check-out times, never your SpO₂, pulse rate, doctors or appointments. Health screening is optional and never affects your attendance or access.
